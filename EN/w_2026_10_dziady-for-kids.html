👻🌙 LITTLE GHOSTS, DZIADY – POLISH FRIGHTS! 🎃✨

We invite children and the whole family to a unique afternoon filled with magic and a gentle thrill! 🌲🔥

Exciting activities await you: children's animations led by Wioletta Falba, great music and fun with DJ LUMAX, alongside the mysterious atmosphere of Polish legends, spirits, forest creatures, and ancient folklore. 👻🧙‍♀️🌿

🔥 There will be games, music, and lots of surprises!

🎁 Every child will receive a nice gift.

  • 📅 Date: October 25, 2026
  • ⏰ Time: 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM
  • 📍 Location: Victoria Sports Center, Rue Léon Autrique 4, 1081 Koekelberg
  • 🎟️ Admission: €10 per child

👻 Come along… if you dare!

Because on this autumn evening, the forest might hide more than meets the eye! 🌙🌲🔥
