WHO WE ARE

KROK is a Polish association operating in Belgium dedicated to creating a vibrant space for culture, education, integration, and community engagement.

We bring together people, ideas, and experiences, developing initiatives tailored for children, youth, families, and the wider community. Our goal is to keep Polish culture present, alive, and accessible for future generations, while making KROK a welcoming hub for cross-border dialogue and collaboration.

WHAT WE DO

CULTURE

Promoting Polish culture, traditions, history, art, and national heritage.

EDUCATION

Organizing educational initiatives and workshops for children, youth, and adults.

CHILDREN & YOUTH

Supporting youth development and delivering enriching learning experiences.

INTEGRATION

Building bridges and meeting spaces for the diaspora and local communities.

SOCIAL PROJECTS

Carrying out projects that address the genuine needs of our community.

COLLABORATION

Connecting non-profits, businesses, schools, and cultural institutions.
