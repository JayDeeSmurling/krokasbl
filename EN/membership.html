Membership

Membership

Join KROK and co-create Polish culture, education, and community in Belgium.

Membership in CENTRE DE LA CULTURE POLONAISE KROK ASBL offers the opportunity to actively participate in the life of the association, support its mission, and co-create initiatives aimed at children, youth, families, and the Polish community in Belgium.

Members of KROK are an essential part of our organization. Thanks to their dedication, ideas, knowledge, and experience, we can continue developing further cultural, educational, and social initiatives.

WHO CAN BECOME A MEMBER?

Any natural person or legal entity may become a member of KROK if they:

  • are interested in the aims and activities of the association,
  • undertake to abide by the bylaws (statutes) of KROK
  • submit a written application for admission as a member

According to the bylaws, the admission of a new member is decided by the General Assembly (Assemblée générale) by a two-thirds majority vote

Founding members are effective members of the association.

WHAT DOES MEMBERSHIP IN KROK ENTAIL?

Membership provides the opportunity to actively participate in the activities of the association and to exercise the rights granted to members under applicable laws and the KROK bylaws

As a member, you can, among other things:

  • participate in the life and activities of the association,
  • support the statutory goals of KROK,
  • get involved in organizing events and projects,
  • propose ideas and initiatives
  • share your knowledge, experience, and skills,
  • participate in cultural, educational, and community initiatives
  • co-create initiatives aimed at children, youth, families, and the Polish community in Belgium,
  • exercise member rights specified in the bylaws and legal regulations.

MEMBER RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS

Members of KROK enjoy full rights granted to members by applicable legal regulations and the association's bylaws.

Each member agrees to respect the bylaws and act in accordance with the aims and guiding principles of KROK.

Members bear no personal liability for commitments made on behalf of the association

MEMBERSHIP FEE

Effective members may be required to pay an annual membership fee.

According to the bylaws, the maximum annual contribution is €100.

The current amount of the fee and the payment rules are determined in accordance with the association's applicable decisions and regulations.

HOW TO BECOME A MEMBER OF KROK?

  1. Fill out the membership form
    Submit a written application to join KROK.
  2. Review the bylaws available for download at the bottom of the page
    Before applying, please read the association's bylaws and the conditions of membership.
  3. The application is presented to the General Assembly
    In accordance with the bylaws, member admission is decided by the General Assembly with a two-thirds majority vote.
  4. Join KROK's activities upon admission
    Once admitted, you can actively participate in the life of the association and support the fulfillment of its goals.

RESIGNATION FROM MEMBERSHIP

A member may resign from membership at any time by submitting a written notice of resignation to the governing body of KROK.

The bylaws also lay down the rules regarding loss of membership and member expulsion.

TOGETHER WE CREATE KROK You do not need prior experience in community activism. You can contribute your time, knowledge, ideas, experience, or skills.

Every contribution makes a difference.
