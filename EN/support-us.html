Your Support Shapes Culture

We operate as a non-profit organization (ASBL). Every donation – regardless of its size – allows us to organize workshops, concerts, exhibitions, and integration gatherings in Belgium.

Where Does Your Support Go?

We care about transparency – every contribution directly supports our statutory objectives.

🎨

Workshops and Education

Purchasing educational and art materials for children, youth, and creative workshop participants.

🎭

Public Events

Venue hire, sound engineering, logistics, as well as honoraria for artists and speakers invited from Poland and Belgium.

🏛️

Growth of the KROK Space

Maintaining the technical and organizational facilities essential to running continuous operations.
