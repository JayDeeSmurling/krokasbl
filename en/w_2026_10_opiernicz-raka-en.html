O!piernicz Raka! 🍪🎗️

📅 17/10/2026 • ✍️ KROK ASBL Team • ⏱️ 2 min read

🎗️ SWEET HELP WITH IMMENSE POWER! ❤️

For the second time, we join forces to demonstrate that even far away from Poland, we can make a difference and change the world for the better! This year, the “O!piernicz Raka!” campaign at our school is organized by the Centre de la Culture Polonaise KROK ASBL.

🍪 We will be selling festive gingerbread cookies to support those who need it most. All proceeds will be donated to the “O!PIERNICZ RAKA” Association, affiliated with the University Children's Clinical Hospital (UDSK) in Białystok.

Event Details:

  • 📅 Date: November 28, 2026 (Saturday)
  • ⏰ Time: 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • 📍 Location: Courtyard of the Joachim Lelewel Polish School at the Embassy of the Republic of Poland in Brussels
  • 🍪 Goal: Supporting young oncology patients at UDSK in Białystok

❤️ Parents, join us!

Buy a gingerbread cookie, support our initiative, and show your child that doing good begins with small acts of kindness. Every purchase is a brick laid toward our shared goal. Because sometimes a tiny gingerbread cookie can bring truly immense hope. 🎗️

Together we can do more!
