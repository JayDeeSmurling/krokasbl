Culture, development, education, communication

Connecting people. Cultivating Polish culture. Creating space for children, youth, and adults.

OUR MISSION

Connecting people, culture, and possibilities.

The mission of the Polish Cultural Centre KROK ASBL is to build an active, open, and integrated community of Polish people in Belgium and to create a space where Polish culture, language, tradition, and history can be nurtured and passed on to future generations.

We believe that culture has the power to bring people together. That is why we create events, educational projects, and community initiatives that foster integration, develop interests, support children, youth, and adults, and encourage active participation in local community life.

We want everyone — regardless of age, background, or experience — to find their place, people, and inspiration to take action at KROK.

KROK is not just events. It is people, relationships, education, culture, and joint action.

OUR VISION

We want to create a recognizable and open centre for Polish culture in Belgium that will serve as a meeting place, a space for growth, and a hub for cooperation — both for Polish people and for anyone interested in Poland and its culture.

Our ambition is to foster partnerships with schools, community organizations, public institutions, local governments, and international partners. We aim to create projects that reach beyond a single community, demonstrating that cultural diversity can be a source of mutual understanding, respect, and new opportunities.

We want KROK to be a place where ideas turn into actions, and actions bring real value to the community.

We believe you don't need to take giant leaps to achieve great things.

All it takes is taking the first STEP.

Our Values

RESPECT

Serves as the foundation of relationships in a multicultural environment, allowing us to build a welcoming and safe space for every member of the Polish diaspora and the local community in Belgium.

COLLABORATION

Reflects the conviction that the most valuable initiatives are born from joining forces with associations, schools, institutions, and businesses around a shared purpose.

RESPONSIBILITY

Ensures reliability, professionalism, and dedication to the highest standards in carrying out projects, caring for participants, and managing the trust placed in us.

OPENNESS

Builds bridges across borders, inviting dialogue and joint action among people with diverse experiences, ideas, and perspectives.

EDUCATION

Fulfills our mission of providing enriching development programs and workshops that support children, youth, and adults in acquiring knowledge and new skills.

TRADITION

Nurtures Polish heritage, history, and language, keeping our native culture alive and close to heart for future generations growing up abroad.

GROWTH

Embodies the meaning of KROK (Step) as a symbol of constantly moving forward, broadening horizons, and continuously refining our initiatives.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT

Responds to genuine human needs, bringing the community closer together and inspiring active, selfless engagement for the common good.
