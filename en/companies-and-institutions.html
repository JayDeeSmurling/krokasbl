Support from Companies and Institutions

Your company can make a real difference with us. KROK creates projects and initiatives that bring together culture, education, personal growth, and community integration. We run activities dedicated to children, youth, families, as well as the Polish and local communities in Belgium. We believe that cooperation between a non-profit organization and the business sector can bring mutual value, especially to the people for whom we create our initiatives.

How can your company support KROK?

Meet our partners

💶
Financial Support

You can provide funding towards the statutory activities of KROK or support a specific project, event, or initiative. Every contribution allows us to grow our operations and bring more ideas to life.

🎁
In-Kind Support

Your company can donate products, materials, equipment, or other goods needed to carry out our activities.

🛠️
Service or Expertise Support

Not all support needs to be financial. You can help KROK by offering your services, professional expertise, practical experience, or specialized skills.

🎪
Event or Project Support

You can become a supporting sponsor for a specific project, workshop, event, or initiative organized by KROK. Together, we can determine the scope and nature of cooperation best suited to the venture.

🤝
Partnership

If your company wishes to engage with KROK on a long-term basis, we welcome the opportunity to discuss a partnership.

A partnership can encompass joint projects, educational and social initiatives, community events, and other efforts aligned with both parties' goals.

Support or Sponsorship?

You can become a supporting partner of a specific project, workshop, event, or initiative organized by KROK. Together, we can establish the scope and format of collaboration suited to the initiative.

Depending on the arrangement, cooperation can take the form of a donation, in-kind contribution, pro bono services, sponsorship, or long-term partnership.

Each collaboration has unique characteristics. That is why we establish the framework, scope, and documentation requirements individually with every organization before starting. For promotional or sponsorship agreements, mutual deliverables are defined case by case.
