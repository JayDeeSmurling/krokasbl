Polish Flag in Koekelberg

📅 11/11/2026 • ✍️ KROK ASBL Team • ⏱️ 2 min read
Update • 12/11/2026 Event Recap

🇵🇱 How We Celebrated November 11 in Koekelberg! 🕊️✨

November 11 – a day that unites Poles, no matter where in the world they live.

With pride and deep emotion, we had the honor of participating in the ceremonies marking Poland's National Independence Day, organized in the Belgian municipality of Koekelberg.

It was a truly special moment in which the white-and-red colors, the sounds of the Polish national anthem, and the remembrance of our Homeland's history brought together representatives of diverse communities – both Polish and Belgian.

It was a particular privilege for our association, the Polish Cultural Centre KROK ASBL, to take part in this ceremony by laying flowers beneath the Polish flag. This symbolic gesture was an expression of respect for Poland's history, the generations who fought for its freedom, and all those who cherish their Polish identity abroad.

We extend our heartfelt thanks to the Municipality of Koekelberg for the invitation, openness, and creating a welcoming space to celebrate a holiday so dear to Poles together.

We also express our gratitude to the Consul of the Republic of Poland, Piotr Adamiuk, for his presence and shared commemoration of this special day, as well as to the Janusz Korczak Polish School in Brussels for nurturing Polish history, traditions, and patriotism among the younger generation.

Thank you to all the organizers, participants, and everyone involved in preparing this ceremony.

For KROK, this was not only an opportunity to attend official commemorations, but above all a meaningful experience and a great distinction. We believe that building relationships between the Polish diaspora and the local Belgian community holds tremendous value, and moments like these remind us that Poland is wherever Polish hearts beat.

May the memory of our history, respect for our national symbols, and a sense of community stay with us every single day.

Thank you for allowing us to be part of this extraordinary event.

📌 Original Announcement (06/11/2026)

🇵🇱 Polish Flag in Koekelberg – Let's Celebrate National Independence Day Together! 🕊️✨

November 11 is a special date for every Pole. On this day, we commemorate Poland's regaining of independence and pay tribute to all those who fought for our Homeland's freedom.

On this occasion, we warmly invite the Polish community and all friends of Poland to take part in the ceremony of raising the white-and-red flag, organized by the municipal authorities of Koekelberg. Join us on November 11 at 12:30 PM at Place Vanhuffel in Koekelberg! 📍

During the ceremony, we will sing the Polish national anthem together, and representatives of our association CENTRE DE LA CULTURE POLONAISE KROK ASBL will lay flowers at the flagpole, paying homage to our history, our heroes, and all generations of compatriots. Attendees will include the Mayor of Koekelberg Olivia P'Tito, Consul of the Republic of Poland Piotr Adamiuk, and students from the Janusz Korczak Polish School.

🍫 Following the official ceremony, a sweet treat awaits all participants: a chocolate tasting hosted by the Belgian Chocolate Village. It is a wonderful opportunity to talk, connect, and celebrate together in a warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Let's be there together! Let's showcase our unity, pride, and devotion to our Homeland, keeping our roots and national identity close to heart. 🇵🇱❤️

KROK ASBL – bringing people together, cherishing Polish traditions, and creating memories that last for a lifetime.

One step, thousands of possibilities!
