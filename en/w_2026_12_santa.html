Saint Nicholas Day with KROK ASBL – In Search of the Lost Star!

On December 5, 2026, we warmly invite you to a special Saint Nicholas Day celebration at Salle Victoria in Koekelberg!

The holiday season is a time of joy, closeness, and sharing unforgettable moments together. To mark the occasion, KROK ASBL is organizing a heartwarming gathering for children and their families, filled with festive cheer, fun activities, and plenty of surprises.

This year, the children will become the heroes of a magical story. The Christmas star has gone missing, and Saint Nicholas needs help! To bring back the holiday magic, young adventurers will set out on: 🌟The Elves' Trail🌟– in search of the lost star!

An interactive adventure awaits the children, where they will unveil each chapter of this festive tale, complete quests, solve riddles, and tackle fun challenges together.

This won't be an ordinary Saint Nicholas party! It's a wonderful chance to spark imagination, build teamwork, share emotions, and be part of an enchanting holiday story.

Our mission is to create a welcoming space where children can experience the true magic of the season, and families can spend quality time together in an atmosphere of warmth, community, and kindness.

Event Details:

  • 📅 Date: December 5, 2026 (Saturday)
  • ⏰ Time: From 3:00 PM
  • 📍 Location: Salle Victoria, Koekelberg
  • 🎟️ Admission: €10

Registration

Spaces are limited. You can register via:
  • 📞 Phone: 0483 67 25 24
  • 📧 Email: contact@krokasbl.org

We warmly invite Polish families in Belgium to come celebrate together! Get ready for a festive adventure, children's laughter, and moments that will stay with us for a long time.
