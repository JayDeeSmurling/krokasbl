Dziady for kids

📅 25/10/2026 • ✍️ Équipe KROK ASBL • ⏱️ 10 min reading

👻 Duszki Dziady – Polish Spooks – That's how it was! 🕯️🍂

On October 25, Sala Victoria in Koekelberg transformed into a magical realm of Slavic legends, mysterious spirits, and extraordinary adventures! ✨

Together, we created an event full of joy, laughter, and unforgettable moments. The youngest participants were welcomed by the mysterious Ghost Trail, the extraordinary Chamber of Destiny featuring children's prophecies, and an encounter with the fairy Wioletta Falba, who transported us into the world of ancient lore and forest creatures. 🌿🔮

There was also plenty of music, dance, and collective fun with DJ LUMAX! Meanwhile, traditional Slavic dishes and drinks allowed us to immerse ourselves even deeper into the festive atmosphere of ancient customs.

❤️ A huge thank you to everyone who joined us! Thank you for your presence, your smiles, your commitment, and for spending this time together. It is each and every one of you who builds the wonderful ambiance that makes our community gatherings truly special.

Special thanks go to our animators, partners, sponsor, and all the volunteers and contributors who helped make this event happen. Thanks to your support, we brought something truly remarkable to life!

📸 We warmly invite you to explore the photo gallery and relive these heartwarming moments. You might even spot yourself in the photos!

KROK ASBL – bringing people together, cherishing Polish traditions, and creating memories that last for a lifetime. 🇵🇱❤️
